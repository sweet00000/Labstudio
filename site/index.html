LabStudio

Browser-based engineering simulators that share one project, units and geometry model.

  • MirrorlabMirror optics ray tracing: exact plane, spherical and parabolic surfaces, pupil and eyebox analysis, parameter sweeps. WebGL 2.
  • Splat TunnelScan or mesh into a GPU lattice-Boltzmann wind tunnel, then export as CAD. WebGPU.
  • Planned: shared CAD workspace, general fluid mechanics, Monte Carlo particle transport. See ROADMAP.md.
